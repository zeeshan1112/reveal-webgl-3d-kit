01 · The Conversion Dilemma

What Should Be Gated vs. Open?

Three high-value touchpoints define the CardSpends journey. Putting a login wall on the wrong one kills top-of-funnel growth.

Touchpoint 1

Explore & Filter

39+ Indian credit cards across 17 banks, 6 core categories, and everyday spend drill-downs.

Touchpoint 2

Side-by-Side Compare

Real-time comparison matrix for fees, SmartBuy/transfer ratios, lounges, and forex markups.

Touchpoint 3

Apply Online

Peak-intent moment when a user chooses a card and clicks through to the issuing bank.

If users are forced to sign up just to read public card terms, bounce rates exceed 75–85% and search engines cannot index our directory.
